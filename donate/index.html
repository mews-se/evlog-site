Where it actually goes

Running EVLog costs 99 USD a year for the Apple Developer Program, which is what it takes to put a free app on the App Store at all. Anything beyond that is a thank you, not a budget.

And the project underneath

EVLog only shows what TeslaMate has already collected. That project does the hard part — talking to the car, making sense of the data, and keeping up with Tesla's changes for years. EVLog would not exist without it.
